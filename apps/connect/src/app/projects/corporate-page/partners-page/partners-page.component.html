PDBe-KB data

Here you can find PDBe-KB resource statistics on data deposited.

  • Click on “Number of protein by resource” to view a barchart showing the total number of proteins with annotations from all the PDBe-KB resources. These resources are further grouped based on type of annotations.
  • Click on “Number of proteins by annotation type” to view a barchart showing the total number of proteins with annotations in these groups.
  • Click on “Last update for each resource” to view when each PDBe-KB resource data was last updated.
