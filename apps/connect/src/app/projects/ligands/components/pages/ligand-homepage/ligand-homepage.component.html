Knowledge Base for Ligands

Background

PDBe-KB is an open, collaborative consortium of leading specialist data resources that provide functional, biochemical, and biophysical annotations for macromolecular structures. Its aim is to facilitate access to structural data by aggregating all information in a knowledge graph for specific biological entities, including proteins, ligands, complexes, and domains.

Initially launched with the aggregated "protein view" webpages in March 2019, PDBe-KB has now developed a novel "ligand view" to centralise small molecule data within the PDB. These ligand-centric pages, keyed on PDB ligand identifiers such as CCD, PRD, and CLC IDs, provide a comprehensive overview of each ligand's chemical, structural, interaction, and functional information to support research in drug discovery, molecular biology, systems biology, structural biology, and pharmacology.

Our goals

  • Enrich PDB Small-Molecule Data: Expand and improve the small-molecule information available in the PDB.
  • Make Ligand Data FAIR: Ensure ligand data is Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR).
  • Enhance Biological Context: Aggregate ligand-related data from PDB entries into a single view, incorporating various annotations to enrich the information within relevant biological systems.
